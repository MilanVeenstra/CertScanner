# QA audit of the incumbent build (2026-10-11)

_Role: QA engineer. Build: `dist-e2e` and `dist` at commit 3957a00 (clean tree), Chromium 141.0.7390.37 headless.
The panel was opened in the harness popup window, not the real side panel. PriceCharting was routed to the repo
fixtures (only the Charizard #199 search answers)._

**Outcome:** 3 P1, 8 P2 and several P3 issues. No `pageerror`s; the only console errors were expected network
failures (`ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED` in the offline test, `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED` for the blocked api.psacard.com after
saving a PSA key).

## P1 – broken

1. **Settings (release `dist`): a required site can't be removed.** "Sites with full access" lists
   `www.pricecharting.com`, a required manifest host permission, and says "You allowed these through 'Sharper scans' or
   'Always allow'", which is false. Remove always fails; the only feedback is the hidden live region.
   Fix: leave manifest-required origins out of the list (or label them "Required for prices" without Remove); show
   failures visibly.
2. **Confirm is a dead end.** After pasting `ambiguous-10digit.png` the only buttons are Show larger, Open cert page
   and Copy. "Scan this page" and "Other ways to scan" are hidden, Escape does nothing and reloading the panel brings
   Confirm back. Fix: add "Scan something else" that returns to Idle.
3. **Privacy copy contradicts what the extension does.** Settings says "CertScanner never fetches prices itself", but
   the panel fetches PriceCharting search pages. About says "A cert number only leaves your device when the grader's
   page opens", but with a PSA key the number goes to `api.psacard.com`. Fix: list exactly what goes to PriceCharting,
   PSA and eBay.

## P2 – confusing

4. **Focus is lost after manual entry.** `activeElement` is BODY and the panel stays scrolled down (heading off-screen).
   Fix: focus the task heading and scroll to the top.
5. **BGS handling is thin.** "BGS 0012345678" opens the generic lookup and relies on the clipboard; the prices block
   shows "Card name not read" twice and says "not read from the label" for a typed number; the eBay hint says "this card
   in this grade" when there is neither. Typing "cgc 1234567890" ignores the CGC prefix.
6. **CGC comics are treated as cards.** "Prices for this card", "Card", grade "CGC · any grade", and the editor is
   auto-expanded with 25 grade options.
7. **Confirm messages contradict each other.** A "Grader unknown" chip next to "Probably CGC Comics" (preselected);
   "The photo is small or unclear" for a clean 544×850 image; a dark-red primary button that reads as danger.
8. **"Population report" for PSA** opens the generic `psacard.com/pop` and silently copies the card name.
9. **Page-scan results:** rows not in page order; the foreign-QR warning appears twice; a row says "Read from barcode"
   while its note says "Taken from the QR code"; no in-panel prices per row.
10. **Changing the price grade relabels it** ("Current price · Grade 9"); the list offers half grades PriceCharting
    has no column for.
11. **The live region keeps old text** after later state changes.

## P3 – polish

- At 360 px the cert URL wraps mid-number and "Current price · PSA 10" wraps as "PSA / 10".
- Sale titles are cut off and can't be expanded.
- Heavy focus box on the task heading after scans.
- Recent scans repeats the current result directly under it.
- A dropped SVG says "The file may be damaged" instead of "format not supported".
- Prices show "Try again" offline but don't retry on reconnect.
- The footer repeats the About disclaimer.
- Dutch: PriceCharting grade labels stay English; in-panel prices are always US dollars.

## Passed

Copy, Open again, >15 MB, PDF, non-slab image, offline note, Settings focus and Back, PSA key validation, history off,
keyboard order with visible rings and accessible names, release build loads/scans/prices, Dutch UI without horizontal
overflow.

## Not verified

Real toolbar icon, Alt+Shift+S, right-click and the real side-panel surface; screen-reader output (ARIA tree only);
zoom, reduced motion and contrast measurement; live sites.

## Information architecture observations

- **Idle:** "Scan this page" is clearly primary, but the shortcut is printed twice and "Other ways to scan" is open by
  default, so upload and manual entry compete with the primary action.
- **Result:** one long column (identity → Copy/Open again → Edit → disclaimer → card name and grade chip → price cards →
  grade grid → sales → five outbound links → eBay disclaimer → Other ways → Recent). The card name is buried under
  "Prices for this card", the disclaimer sits between the actions and the prices, eBay links are split between buttons
  and text links, and grader-specific gaps are shown with card wording.
- **Confirm:** reasons, thumbnail with a second grader indication, the edit form and Recent, but no way out.
- **Page results:** summary, a global warning repeating a row note, rows; prices missing.
- **Settings:** clear sections; "Sites with full access" is wrong in release; the eBay site setting is under "Prices &
  listings" but only affects eBay links.

Screenshots and scripts were kept outside the repo (session scratchpad).
